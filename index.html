02 / What agentic means

A brief becomes
a build-and-review loop.

An AI model supplies reasoning and generation. An agent adds project context and tools so it can carry out a bounded development task.

01

02

Plan

03

Build

Code, preview and automated checks

04

Review

Human feedback, revision and approval

Repeat in small increments. A convincing preview is only one part of a finished product.

09 / A proposed 90-day transition

Start small.
Prove it. Then expand.

Introduce the approach through a controlled pilot while maintaining existing client commitments.

01

Days 1–30 · Prepare

Select one low-risk project. Record current delivery effort, prepare the design system and train a small team.

Exit: approved brief + ready team
02

Days 31–60 · Pilot

Build one complete journey in small increments. Review design fidelity, defects, rework and total cost weekly.

Exit: tested pilot + retrospective
03

Days 61–90 · Expand

Refine reusable patterns and delivery checklists. Add a web-app workflow; scope a mobile proof of concept.

Exit: evidence-based scale decision

10 / Keep speed meaningful

Measure the whole job.
Protect the standard.

Faster implementation is the goal. The pilot must establish whether total delivery time and cost actually improve.

Track against a baseline

  • Time from approved design to accepted release
  • Engineering, review and rework hours
  • Defects found before and after launch
  • Design acceptance and user-task completion
  • Total cost, including AI usage and hosting

Keep human release gates

  • Design approval against agreed screens
  • Code review and workflow test evidence
  • Permissions and sensitive-data checks
  • Client acceptance and an identified owner
  • Deployment rollback and support plan

Set targets after measuring the baseline. Do not promise a fixed speed-up before the pilot.
